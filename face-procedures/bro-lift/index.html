Bro Lift

Confident Lift. Masculine Balance.

Specialized Brow Lift Solutions for Men Seeking a Stronger, More Refreshed Appearance — Only at ReevanaX.

What is a Bro Lift?

The Bro Lift is a specialized brow lift procedure tailored for men. Unlike traditional brow lifts that may create a higher, more arched brow (typically preferred by women), the Bro Lift maintains a straighter, lower-set brow while correcting heaviness, wrinkles, and droop that contribute to a tired or stern appearance.
This treatment is:
  • Tailored for male facial anatomy
  • Focused on subtle, natural improvements
  • Designed to maintain masculine features and expressions
  • Long-lasting and discreet
Why You Might Need a Bro Lift
As men age, the brow tends to descend — making you look tired, angry, or older than you feel. You may benefit from a Bro Lift if you notice:
  • Low or heavy brows
  • Deep horizontal forehead creases
  • Glabellar lines (the “11s” between the eyes)
  • Hooded upper eyelids or a “closed-off” look
  • A fatigued or intense expression even when relaxed
At ReevanaX, we understand the nuances of male aesthetics. The Bro Lift is subtle, sculpted, and strength-enhancing — not dramatic or feminizing.
How a Bro Lift Works

1. Facial Analysis & Goal Setting

We begin with a consultation focused on your brow position, skin elasticity, hairline, and goals. We design a lift that strengthens your natural expression — never alters it.

2. Male-Specific Surgical Approach

Your provider may recommend one of the following techniques:
  • Endoscopic Bro Lift – Minimally invasive, using small incisions behind the hairline and a camera to adjust brow positioning with minimal downtime.
  • Temporal Bro Lift – Subtle elevation at the outer brow to reduce heaviness without changing central brow position.
  • Direct Brow Lift – An option for men with receding hairlines or strong forehead lines, using an incision at the upper brow edge.
  • Adjunctive Treatments – Combine with Botox or dermal fillers to soften lines or refresh tired eyes.

3. Recovery & Results:

Most male patients return to work or regular activities within 7–10 days. Final results appear gradually over 4–6 weeks and can last several years.
