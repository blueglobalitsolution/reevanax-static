## The Modern Groom: Why Pre-Wedding Care Matters

Gone are the days when wedding preparations and aesthetic pampering were exclusive to brides. Modern grooms understand that looking radiant, well-groomed, and confident on their wedding day is just as essential. High-definition photography, stage lighting, and close-up video shoots mean your skin and hair must be in peak health.

At **ReevanaX**, we provide customized, scientifically backed pre-wedding aesthetic packages specifically tailored for male skin and hair physiology.

---

## 1. Advanced Hydrafacial & Medi-Facials for Men
Male skin is typically 20-25% thicker than female skin, with larger pores and higher sebum production. Our medical-grade **HydraFacial** deeply cleanses, exfoliates dead cells, extracts blackheads, and infuses antioxidant serums.

* **Ideal Timeline:** Start 2 to 3 months prior to the wedding (3 sessions spaced 3 weeks apart).
* **Key Benefits:** Instant radiance, reduced oiliness, un-clogged pores, and smooth complexion for razor-burn free shaving.

---

## 2. Hair PRP & Growth Factor Therapy
Thinning hair or a receding hairline can cause wedding-day anxiety. Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) and Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) stimulate hair follicles, reverse thinning, and improve hair density naturally.

* **Ideal Timeline:** Start 4 to 6 months before the wedding day for maximum visible density.
* **Key Benefits:** Thicker hair texture, halted hair fall, and enhanced scalp vitality.

---

## 3. Laser Hair Reduction for Beard Line Shaping
Tired of neck irritation and irregular beard lines? Precision diode laser shaping provides a clean, well-defined beard contour that lasts through the honeymoon.

---

## 4. Under-Eye Dark Circle & Rejuvenation Therapy
Wedding planning stress and late-night functions often lead to stubborn dark circles and puffy eyes. Our specialized non-invasive under-eye meso-infusions and laser toning restore a refreshed, energetic look.
